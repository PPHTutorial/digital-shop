COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my download after paying?

Downloads are released instantly on the confirmation screen and saved automatically into your customer account under "My Orders".

What payment methods are supported?

We support Cards (Visa/Mastercard), Mobile Money across Ghana (GHS), Kenya (KES), and Uganda (UGX), plus 300+ cryptocurrencies via NOWPayments.

Can I re-download files in the future?

Yes! Log in with the email address used during purchase to access lifetime file updates and downloads from your account dashboard.

What is the refund policy on digital items?

Due to the irreversible nature of digital downloads, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis if files are corrupted or duplicate payments were made.
