Common Support Solutions

Where do I download my purchases?

Every completed order appears under "Orders" in your account, with a secure download link that stays valid for the signed expiry window.

How can I request a refund?

Digital downloads are generally final, but corrupted files or duplicate charges are reviewed case-by-case — open a ticket with your order reference and we'll take a look.

What payment methods are supported?

Cards, African mobile money (GHS, KES, UGX), and 300+ cryptocurrencies via NOWPayments — all processed securely at checkout.

What happens if a vendor's product is removed?

Anything you already purchased stays in your order history and download vault — removal from the storefront only affects new sales.

Can I re-download files in the future?

Yes — sign in with the email used at checkout to access every past order and its download link from your account dashboard.
