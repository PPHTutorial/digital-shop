A digital product is simply something useful that a person can buy and receive without waiting for a physical package to arrive. It can be a guide, workbook, template, checklist, spreadsheet, set of scripts, prompt pack, lesson, design resource, or a bundle of several useful files.
The good news is that you do not need to build a large company before you can create one. You do not need to know everything about business. You do not need to spend months making a huge course. What you do need is a specific problem, a useful solution, a clear buyer, and enough discipline to finish and publish.
This book is designed around one simple idea: a small, useful product that gets finished is more valuable than a brilliant product idea that stays in your notebook.

Do not read this book like a novel and postpone the work until the end. Use it as a working manual. Each major section is designed to help you make a decision or create an asset. When you see a worksheet, stop and fill it in. When you see a prompt, adapt it. When you see a checklist, use it.
Choose one product idea. Do not start with ten ideas at once.
Define the person the product is for and the problem it solves.
Create the smallest useful version that can deliver a clear result.
Package the product so the buyer understands what they are getting.
Publish the offer, promote it, and start real conversations with potential buyers.
Use buyer questions and feedback to improve the next version.
