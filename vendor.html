WHAT YOU PAY FOR

How boosting is charged

Views — charged per 1,000 times your product is actually seen on screen. Cards nobody scrolls to cost nothing.
Clicks — a flat rate when someone opens your product from a boosted card.
Sales — a small percentage of the order value, charged only when a boosted product actually sells.

The same viewer is only counted once per day per campaign. Spending stops automatically when the budget or the wallet runs out.
