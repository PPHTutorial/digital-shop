Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our curation and safety policies.

How are products verified on DigiStore?

Every item submitted goes through our internal validation process, checking for secure code practices, valid download components, and verified developer identities before it can be published.

What is the lifetime DigiStore Vault storage?

Every purchased asset is saved instantly into your account's order history. If the creator updates the file, you can download the fresh version at no extra charge, any time.

Can I apply to sell my own digital goods?

Yes — you can apply as a vendor from your account. We review every application, then handle checkout, payment verification, and secure delivery on your behalf.
